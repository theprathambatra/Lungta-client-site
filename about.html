LUNGTA

A performance fashion label built around movement, wind and the belief that technical clothing can feel as considered as anything in a wardrobe.

The identity

A system, not a stamp.

The LUNGTA wordmark, Apex mark and Wind Horse all come from one idea: movement with direction. The identity is designed to live on garments, objects and environments without becoming decorative noise.

The symbol

The Wind Horse.

Wind gives it lightness. The horse gives it force. The Apex gives it direction. Together they form the language behind LUNGTA.

The name

Made of momentum.

LUNGTA is built for people who do not separate performance from the rest of life. Training is one part of movement. The garment has to travel further.
